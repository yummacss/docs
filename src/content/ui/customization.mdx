## Use Props

Props are the reliable way to change a component, because the component applies them itself.

```tsx
<Badge tone="subtle" color="green" size="lg" shape="pill">
  Shipped
</Badge>
```

Reach for the table before anything else. Most of what people try to do with `className` already exists as a prop.

## Own Your Components

`yummaui add` copies a real file into your project, and nothing overwrites it later. When props & `className` are not enough, all you need need to do is edit it.

Components are built on [Base UI](https://base-ui.com), so the primitive underneath is documented there, which matters the moment you start editing. Pages built on a primitive link to it directly.

## Colors & Theme

Component colors come from Yumma CSS, so they are configured in `yumma.config.mjs` rather than anywhere in Yumma UI. Adding a brand color or changing how shades are generated is covered in [Colors](/docs/colors) and [Configuration](/docs/configuration).
